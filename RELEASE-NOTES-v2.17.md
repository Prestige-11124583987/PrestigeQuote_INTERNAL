# Release Notes v2.17.0

## Added

- Additional Item(s) editor for accessories/manual line items.
- Each additional item supports Item Name, Price / Ea, Quantity, and a manually entered Discount percentage.
- The app calculates each item total and the total cost of all additional items.
- Quote page 1 now shows an Additional Item(s) total line between Doors / Windows and Installation.

## Calculation

Additional Item retail = Price / Ea × Quantity.
Additional Item discount amount = retail × item discount %.
Additional Item total = retail - discount amount.

Additional items are included in package retail, total savings, quote total, and production deposit basis.
